# 01 - Problem Statement

## Current Problems in Streaming & Discovery

### 1. Decision Fatigue
With over 1.5 million titles available across fragmented streaming platforms, viewers are overwhelmed by choice. Psychological research shows that excessive options lead to paralysis, dissatisfaction, and abandonment of the selection process.

### 2. Doom Scrolling
Users frequently spend upwards of 20–30 minutes browsing thumbnail grids on platforms like Netflix or Hulu. This time spent searching often exceeds the duration of a standard television episode, resulting in frustration and cognitive burnout.

### 3. Bad Recommendations
Platform algorithms are fundamentally misaligned with consumer satisfaction. They prioritize:
*   **In-house originals** over objectively better content.
*   **Licensing priorities** and short-term promotions.
*   **Popularity bias**, recommending mainstream blockbusters while burying niche, high-match titles.

### 4. Generic Genres
Standard classifications like "Sci-Fi," "Comedy," or "Drama" are too broad to be useful. For example, *Interstellar* and *The Matrix* are both categorized as "Sci-Fi," yet they satisfy entirely different narrative, philosophical, and emotional appetites.

### 5. Content Overload
Viewers maintain watchlist notes across disparate apps, spreadsheets, and memory. Without a centralized, intelligent tracking system, forgotten gems and personal viewing goals are lost in the noise.

---

## How Re:Watch Solves Them

### 1. Multi-Dimensional Content Mapping (StoryPrint)
Re:Watch discards surface-level genres. Every title is quantified on granular, emotional, and narrative axes (e.g., *Pacing: Fast*, *Tone: Melancholic*, *Complexity: High*), allowing users to find content that matches their immediate psychological state.

### 2. Evolving User Profiling (TasteDNA)
Rather than relying on simple "thumbs up/down" metrics, Re:Watch generates a living mathematical profile of a user's tastes. It incorporates **time-decay**, ensuring that an obsession with horror films five years ago doesn't skew today's recommendations.

### 3. Explainable AI & Mood Search (Insight AI)
Users can input natural language queries (e.g., *"A quiet, atmospheric film that feels like a rainy Sunday"*). Insight AI maps the query directly to StoryPrint vectors and explains exactly *why* each recommended title was selected.

### 4. Consolidated Tracking & Insightful Analytics
Re:Watch serves as a centralized hub for logging, rating, and analyzing viewing habits—rewarding users with deep personal insights and yearly statistical retrospectives.
