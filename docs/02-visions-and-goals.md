# 02 - Vision and Goals

## Mission
To transform screen entertainment discovery from a frustrating, algorithmically biased chore into an intuitive, explainable, and deeply personalized journey.

## Vision
We envision a future where media platforms serve the viewer's psychological and intellectual appetite rather than corporate syndication goals—making Re:Watch the universal passport for content tracking and recommendation.

## Core Values
1. **User-First Relevance:** Recommendations are optimized strictly for viewer compatibility, never for promotional sponsorship.
2. **Transparency & Explainability:** Every recommendation is accompanied by a human-readable explanation of why the match was made.
3. **Data Sovereignty & Delight:** Users own their watch history and are rewarded with rich, engaging insights into their tastes.
4. **Narrative Respect:** Treat film and television as multi-dimensional art rather than flat genre commodities.

## Success Metrics (KPIs)
*   **Time-to-Play (TTP):** Reduce user browsing time to less than **3 minutes** per session.
*   **Recommendation Acceptance Rate:** Target an acceptance rate of **> 35%** on Insight AI-recommended titles.
*   **User Retention (MAU):** Achieve a **40% Monthly Active User** retention rate after 90 days.
*   **Rating Density:** Average **> 15 logged items** per active user per month.

## Long-Term Goals
*   Establish Re:Watch as the industry standard for content tagging via open-sourcing the StoryPrint taxonomy.
*   Integrate direct webhooks with major streaming platforms (where APIs permit) for automated viewing sync.
*   Grow a community-curated database of StoryPrint vectors validated by consensus.

## Future Expansion
*   **Social Compatibility Scoring:** Allow two or more users to combine their TasteDNAs to generate group recommendations.
*   **Cross-Media Support:** Expand StoryPrint and TasteDNA models to encompass novels, video games, and graphic novels.
*   **Smart TV & Mobile Companion Apps:** Native iOS, Android, and Apple TV / Android TV applications.
